## Caption

To Haarlem. On foot. Carrying fish.

Long before the boulevard and the beach clubs, Zandvoort's fish sellers carried the catch from the beach to the market in Haarlem. It was hard, heavy work, and it helped keep their families going.

Their story is now on show at Zandvoorts Museum in "Zandvoortse Vrouwen", until 4 April 2027. It's a good reason to step off the beach for an hour and see the village behind the coast.

Slide 2: Wybrand Hendriks, watercolour, c. 1800. Noord-Hollands Archief (CC0).
