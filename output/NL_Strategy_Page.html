Language and tone

Dutch first, one line of English

Captions and overlays in Dutch, then one line starting "EN:". The tagline stays in English. We use "je", never "u". A 30-day test (T1) confirms it, and at 100 followers Meta unlocks the audience data to recheck.

Do

  • Concrete facts: €10, 1 jaar geldig, Kerkstraat, 1 kg via CleanHub
  • Plain and direct (nuchter): "Gewoon €10. Een jaar geldig."
  • Real names, streets and people
  • Local references: uitwaaien, strandjutten, Buienradar, groepsapp, NS-trein, lootjes trekken
  • Impact claims exactly as in the brand guide; CleanHub (global) kept apart from Juttersgeluk (local)

Don't

  • Superlatives: "de beste", "ultiem", "verborgen pareltje", "magisch"
  • Fake urgency: countdowns, "laatste kans!"
  • "Lokale favorieten ontdekken" in every caption
  • More than 2 emoji, stacked exclamation marks, "gezellig" more than once a month
  • AI tells: "het is niet X, het is Y", one sentence per line, em dashes in posts

Caption framework

1  HAAK      ≤125 tekens · het moment
2  CONTEXT   wat · waar (straat) · wie
3  LOOKYBOOKY  1 feitelijke zin
4  CTA       Stuur dit naar… · vraag · Bewaar
5  EN:       1 regel
6  #Zandvoort #LookyBooky + 1–2 NL tags

Reel rules

  1. Something happens in frame 1: a hand, a pour, a pick-up. No establishing shots.
  2. 2–6 Dutch words on screen that open a question. Works muted.
  3. Length follows the story until test T3 reports (the best Reel was 15s).
  4. A payoff worth sending: who sends this, to whom, and why?
  5. Original, no watermark, licensed sound, real people.

All Dutch copy is an original draft. A native speaker proofs it before posting.

Format and funnel

Three Reels for discovery, one carousel for saves and follows, Stories on 4–5 days for community and conversion. If production is tight, drop the Monday Reel. A steady 3 a week beats an abandoned 4.

Gifting and giveaways

Passes as gifts, and one giveaway a month

Lootjes trekken for pakjesavond runs on a €10–€20 budget, and the pass is digital, so it works as a last-minute Christmas gift too. Giveaways are a follow tool: they help us reach 100 followers, which unlocks the audience data.

Gifting

  • 3 Dec carousel: "Lootjes getrokken? Budget: €10."
  • New Cadeau Highlight with the gift steps and a UTM link
  • 19 Dec Story: "Last-minute kerstcadeau? De pass is digitaal."
  • Plain tone: price, validity, what the receiver gets. No "perfect gift!!"

Kerstgiveaway: 3 x 2 passes 15–20 Dec

Prize: two passes per winner, one for you and one for who you'd bring.
Enter: follow @lookybooky.nl, tag one person you'd bring, and say where you'd go first. That last step filters out prize hunters.
Format: a Reel co-hosted with one partner as Collab. Stories reminders on 15, 17 and 19 Dec; closes Sun 20 Dec 23:59; winners in Stories on Mon 21 Dec.
Rules: publish actievoorwaarden first (Gedragscode Promotionele Kansspelen): organiser, period, prize, how winners are drawn, free to enter, 18+ or parental consent, privacy. Say it isn't run by Instagram.
Measure: left out of all tests and baselines. Log giveaway follows separately; aim for 70% or more still following after 30 days.

When to act

Three Reels in a row above 65% skip: re-cut openings first. Reach up but followers flat 2 weeks after the profile fix: try a second bio. Sends below 0.5% for a month: more "Herkenbaar aan zee". A missed target means new tactics, not a new target.

Review rhythm

Log every post at 48 hours and 7 days. A 30-minute review every Monday. At 30 days, re-pull vidIQ and re-run the audit script. A full content audit at 90 days.

Experiments

Five tests to run now

With 100–2,000 reach per post, classic A/B significance isn't possible. Each test uses a Trial Reel (the same footage as a second version, shown only to non-followers) or alternating posts, with a decision rule set in advance.
