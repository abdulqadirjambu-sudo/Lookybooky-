In short

Five decisions for December

  1. Fix the profile before 1 December. Name "LookyBooky Zandvoort", a Dutch-first bio with the €10 offer, a tracked link and Highlights. Reach isn't the leak; the profile is.
  2. Show action and process, not atmosphere. Beachcombing was 17% of posts and 41% of reach. Ambient venue posts were a third of posts and a fifth of reach.
  3. Go Dutch first. Dutch captions and on-screen text, one English line. A test runs all month to confirm it.
  4. Four feed posts a week: Reels Mon, Wed, Fri at 18:00 and a carousel on Thursday. Stories on 4–5 days. Rest at Christmas.
  5. Use gifting and one giveaway to turn viewers into followers: the €10 pass as a Sinterklaas and Christmas gift, and a Christmas giveaway on 15–20 December.

What worked

Juttersgeluk beach walk: 1,935 reach, 30% skip rate. "Less screen time, more sea air": 1,182 reach, 31 shares, 8 comments. Filoxenia kitchen: 552 reach, watched 119%. Something happens in each of them.

What didn't

November's 8-second mood clips (median reach 118), "Save this" on content nobody saves (0 carousel saves), weekend and midday slots, English brand statements (0 organic shares).

24 posts, 2 of them half of all reach, so read this as direction, not proof. Four posts show shares above reach (likely internal DMs) and are left out of the baselines. Meta withholds audience demographics below 100 followers.

Strategy

Five content pillars

Kijk beter aan zee

Look closer at the coast · signature · 30%

Beachcombing, what the sea brings in, the winter coast in action, impact you can see.

Van dichtbij bij lokale adressen

Up close at local spots · 25%

Hands, process and people behind the counter. Replaces ambient venue posts.

Het dorp achter de boulevard

The village behind the boulevard · 15%

Heritage, streets, archive photos, then and now. Best follow rate on the account.

Herkenbaar aan zee

So Zandvoort · 15%

Wind, uitwaaien, Buienradar, the gull at your table. Brought 2 of our 3 recorded follows.

Slim uit in Zandvoort

A smart day out · conversion · max 15%

How the pass works, open in winter, day trips, gifts, giveaways. Mostly in Stories.

Retire

 

Brand statements, single images, one-line mood clips where nothing happens.

Language and tone

  • Dutch caption and overlays, then one line starting "EN:". Tagline stays English.
  • "je", never "u". Plain and direct: "Gewoon €10. Een jaar geldig."
  • Real names and streets; local words: uitwaaien, strandjutten, Buienradar, lootjes trekken.
  • No superlatives, countdowns or "laatste kans". Max 2 emoji.
  • Impact claims exactly as in the brand guide. CleanHub (global) stays separate from Juttersgeluk (local).
  • A native speaker proofs all Dutch copy before posting.
