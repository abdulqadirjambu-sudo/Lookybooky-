# LookyBooky Zandvoort: Netherlands Social Media Strategy

**Account:** @lookybooky.nl · **Market:** Zandvoort (Noord-Holland) and its day-trip catchment (Haarlem, Amsterdam)
**Horizon:** 24 Nov 2026 – 21 Feb 2027 (30/60/90 days) · **Companion files:** `Next_30_Days_Calendar.csv`, `Phase2_Audit.md`
**Skills applied:** instagram-growth, content-audit, content-pillars, content-strategy, social, hook-writer, copywriting, marketing-psychology, seasonal-and-moment-marketing, goals-and-kpis, ab-testing, brand-voice-enforcement (against brand guidelines v3.25)

---

## 0. The diagnosis in four lines

1. **Instagram already shows LookyBooky to strangers:** 99.3% of reach is non-followers. Reach is not the main constraint.
2. **The profile doesn't convert them.** 268 profile visits in 90 days produced 7 link taps and an account of 32 followers. **Fix the profile first.**
3. **Reach depends on hits.** Reels with a skip rate under 55% average 2× the reach of the rest. The winners show **action with a purpose, process and a relatable contrast**. The losers show **ambient atmosphere**.
4. **The account speaks only English to a market that is mostly Dutch.** All 24 captions are in English, the town is ~85% Dutch and the brand guide is written in Dutch.

Everything below follows from these four points.

## 2. Language and tone guidelines

### Language rule: Dutch first, one line of English

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Caption** | Dutch hook and body, then **one line starting "EN:"** with a summary. Never two full captions. |
| **On-screen text** | Dutch, 2–6 words per card. **Bilingual on screen** only when the post targets internationals (train from Amsterdam, uitwaaien) or explains a Dutch word. |
| **Brand tagline** | Keep "Explore more. Spend less. Make an impact." in English as the brand signature, on end cards and in sign-offs only. |
| **Hashtags** | 3–4: `#Zandvoort #LookyBooky` plus 1–2 Dutch topic tags (`#uitwaaien #strandjutten #zandvoortaanzee #dagjeuit`). There's no reach effect (ρ = +0.12), so they're for categorisation only. |
| **Address form** | **je/jij**, never **u**. Informal is standard for Dutch consumer brands, and the Instagram audience is mostly 25–44. |
| **Validation** | The language test (T1) runs for 30 days. When the account reaches **100 followers**, Meta unlocks audience city, age and country data; recheck the split then. |

### Voice calibrated for the Netherlands

The brand guide describes the tone as **"Fris in uitstraling, lokaal in selectie, menselijk in toon en doelgericht in impact"** (fresh in look, local in selection, human in tone, purposeful in impact). For Dutch readers that means:

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Be concrete: **€10, 1 jaar geldig, Kerkstraat, 1 kg via CleanHub** | Superlatives: "de beste", "ultiem", "must-visit", "verborgen pareltje" (hidden gem), "magisch" |
| Understate and be direct (nuchter): "Gewoon €10. Een jaar geldig." | Hype or fake urgency: "Laatste kans!", countdowns, "OMG" |
| Name real people, streets and places | Generic phrases: "lokale favorieten ontdekken" in every caption |
| Light self-aware humour ("Betaalt nooit.") | Exclamation-mark stacks, more than 2 emoji per caption |
| Cultural references Dutch readers recognise: **uitwaaien, strandjutten, Buienradar, groepsapp, NS-trein, lootjes trekken, pakjesavond, kortste dag** | Overused clichés: "gezellig" (at most once a month), "Hollandse glorie" |
| State the impact claim exactly as in the brand guide | Merging CleanHub (global) with Juttersgeluk (local), or new numbers that haven't been checked |
| Write in normal paragraphs | AI tells (`social` skill): "het is niet X, het is Y", one sentence per line throughout (broetry), em dashes in posts |

**Claims register.** Only these claims are approved, all from brand guide v3.25:
- "Bij aankoop van een LookyBooky Pass wordt via CleanHub 1 kg ocean-bound waste verwijderd." (Each pass removes 1 kg of ocean-bound waste through CleanHub.)
- "1% van de lokale bestedingen via LookyBooky gaat naar lokale impact- en clean-upprojecten." (1% of local spending through LookyBooky goes to local impact and clean-up projects.)
- Deal types "bijvoorbeeld 10% korting, €10 korting of 2-voor-1" (for example 10% off, €10 off or 2-for-1), always with "verschilt per adres" (varies by venue).

**Dutch copy QA.** All Dutch copy in the calendar is an original draft, not a machine translation. A native speaker should read it before publishing; the connected DeepL Write can serve as a second check.

### Caption framework (fill in for every post)

```
1  HAAK (hook)      ≤125 tekens; the moment, taken from the actual footage (hook-writer)
2  CONTEXT          1–2 zinnen: wat · waar (straat) · wie
3  LOOKYBOOKY       1 feitelijke zin (no superlatives)
4  CTA              "Stuur dit naar…" (send) · specifieke vraag · "Bewaar dit" (save; utility only)
5  EN:              1 regel
6  #Zandvoort #LookyBooky + 1–2 Dutch topic tags
```

### Reel build rules

1. **Something happens in frame 1:** a hand, a pour, a pick-up, a gull. No establishing shots; the target skip rate is under 55%.
2. A Dutch overlay of 2–6 words that opens a question; it must work with sound off.
3. **Length follows the story.** The 6–10s rule is suspended until test T3 reports, because the best Reel was 15s.
4. The payoff is something worth sending. Ask: "who sends this, to whom, and why?"
5. Optional 1s end card: turquoise mark, "Explore more. Spend less. Make an impact."
6. Must be original, with no watermark, a licensed sound and real people (brand guide photography rules).

## 3. Format and funnel mix

| Funnel job | Format | Per week | Days and time (CET) | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Reels | **3** | Mon, Wed, Fri **18:00** (Wed is 10:00 on test weeks) | Non-follower reach at 7 days, skip rate, sends per reach |
| **Consideration, saves, follows** | Carousels | **1** | Thu **18:00** | Saves and follows per 100 reach |
| **Community and conversion** | Stories (2–4 frames, at least 1 sticker) | **4–5 days** | 12:00 and/or 19:00 on non-Reel days; reshare each Reel the same day | Sticker response rate, link taps per story reach |
| **Borrowed reach** | Partner Collab co-author posts | **1** (half of venue Reels, alternated for T4) | On a Reel day | Reach compared with tag-only posts, follows |
| **Retention** | Community block | Daily 15 min, plus Sunday | After each post and on Sunday | Reply time under 24h |

**Ratio:** feed is **75% Reels and 25% carousels**, no single images. That's 4 feed posts a week, up from about 3 in November. If production capacity is tight, the Monday Reel is the one to drop. A steady 3 a week beats an abandoned 4 (`content-calendar`).

**Why 18:00:** the account's own evening posts have a median reach of 182 (n=13), against 53 for midday (n=3). Metricool's general curve peaks at 18:00 and 10:00 on weekdays, and the 10:00 slot is tested in T5. Weekends get Stories only, because the general curve halves on Saturday and Sunday and both weekend feed posts so far were among the weakest.

### Collaboration plan
- **Partners to rotate:** Thalassa, De Haven van Zandvoort, Filoxenia, Sweet Pearl, Mel's, Juttersgeluk, Zandvoorts Museum, CleanHub.
- **Ask each partner for:** (1) Collab co-author acceptance, (2) 20 minutes of filming access for a process shot, (3) a reshare to their Story on posting day.
- **Give each partner:** the raw clip for their own use and a monthly "what your post did" line (reach and sends). Showing them results makes them more willing to collaborate again.
- **Reach outside partners:** @visitzandvoort and local entrepreneurs, through comments and Story reshares. No engagement pods.

## 6. KPIs: targets for 30, 60 and 90 days

Built with `goals-and-kpis` (NORTH): one North Star, four supporting KPIs, each set from the real baseline. Read Reel numbers at **7 days** so older and newer posts compare fairly.

| KPI | Baseline (source) | 30 days (23 Dec) | 60 days (22 Jan) | 90 days (21 Feb) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ★ **North Star: median non-follower reach per Reel (7-day)** | 182 overall; **118 in Nov** | **250** | **350** | **500** |
| Median Reel skip rate | 65% (Nov 67%) | 58% | 52% | ≤48% |
| Organic sends per reach, Reels (pooled per month) | 1.15% Aug–Nov, **0.24% in Nov** | 1.0% | 1.5% | 2.0% |
| Followers (conversion proxy) | 32 | 55 | **100** (unlocks demographics) | 160 |
| Website link taps per month (plus UTM sessions in analytics) | ~2.3 (7 in 90 days) | 10 | 20 | 30 |
| *Guardrail:* carousel saves per 100 reach | 0 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 1.5 |
| *Guardrail:* internal or partner DM sends logged | not logged | 100% logged | 100% | 100% |

**Action thresholds:**
- **Three Reels in a row with skip rate above 65%:** re-cut openings with action in frame 1 before posting anything else.
- **Reach rises but followers don't move within 2 weeks of the profile fix:** test a second bio version and swap the pinned posts.
- **Sends per reach stays under 0.5% for a month:** move more of the mix to Pillar 4 (Herkenbaar aan zee) and the send CTA.
- **A target is missed:** change tactics first, not the targets.

**Review rhythm:** check every post at 48 hours and 7 days (log it in `data/`). Do a 30-minute review every Monday. At 30 days, re-run `analysis/audit.py` on a fresh vidIQ pull. At 90 days, a full content audit.

## 7. Experiment backlog: 5 tests to start now

At 100–2,000 reach per post, classic significance testing isn't possible (`ab-testing`: underpowered). So each test uses **Instagram Trial Reels** (the same footage as a second version shown only to non-followers) or alternates matched posts. Every test has a **decision rule fixed in advance**. Results are **directional**. Each post carries only one Trial-Reel variable.

| ID | Hypothesis | Design | Primary metric | Guardrail | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 Language** | Dutch overlays and captions lower skip rate and raise sends among Zandvoort and Randstad viewers | 3 pairs (30 Nov, 7 Dec, 18 Dec): main post in NL, Trial Reel in EN with identical cut | Skip rate | Sends per reach | NL becomes the default if it wins skip rate in at least 2 of 3 pairs without losing on sends. If it's tied, stay Dutch-first and recheck when demographics unlock at 100 followers. |
| **T2 Hook** | Action in frame 1 beats a scenic establishing shot | 2 pairs in 30 days (4 Dec, 14 Dec), then 1 more in January: main post action-first, Trial Reel scenic-first | Skip rate | Average watch time (seconds) | Action-first becomes the rule if it wins 2 of 3 with a skip-rate gap of 5 points or more. |
| **T3 Length** | 13–16s cuts beat 7–9s cuts for reach, because the account's best Reel was 15s | 2 pairs (11 Dec, 23 Dec) plus 1 in January: main post vs Trial Reel at a different length | Non-follower reach at 7 days | Skip rate | Set the default length band to the winner of 2 of 3; until then, length follows the footage. |
| **T4 Collab** | A partner as Collab co-author beats a plain tag on reach and follows | Alternate venue Reels: collab (27 Nov, 11 Dec) vs tag only (2 Dec, 16 Dec), then repeat in January | Non-follower reach at 7 days | Follows, partner goodwill | If collab posts reach at least 1.3× tag-only on average, make Collab the default for all venue posts. |
| **T5 Time slot** | 10:00 on weekdays (Metricool's general peak) matches or beats 18:00 (the account's best evidence) | 10:00 Reels on 9 Dec and 21 Dec, compared with the median of the 18:00 Reels in the same weeks | Non-follower reach at 7 days | Comments in the first 2 hours | Keep 18:00 unless both 10:00 posts beat their weekly median. |

**Next in the backlog (days 31–90):** the "Save" CTA on utility vs story carousels · a carousel cover with a number vs a question · a send CTA vs a specific question · a Broadcast Channel launch ("Zandvoort deze week", a weekly local digest) once there are 100 followers · a Facebook cross-post of venue Reels for the older local audience (Facebook is the #2 platform in NL).
